HOW TO PLAY

Survive all 10 waves of the kaiju setlist. Each wave is a turn-based fight; between waves you draft rewards and spend Merch Tokens. Losing all 80 HP ends the run — winning unlocks the next Encore tier.

1. YOUR TURN

  • Draw & play. Draw up to 5 cards (keys 1–5) and spend Hype Energy (3 per turn) to play them. Unspent Energy is lost when the turn ends.
  • Pin one card. The 📌 toggle keeps a card in hand for next turn, but your next draw stops at 5 cards total — so keeping draws one fewer.
  • End the turn (E). The boss then resolves its shown intent. Shield absorbs damage before HP; leftover Shield decays by half (rounded down) into your next turn.
  • Charge Cheer. Playing cards builds Crowd Cheer to 100% to unleash the free Joint Finale (Q). Your once-per-run Leader Ability is R.
  • Buff cards take one of three stage slots. Timed buffs count down and expire when the wave ends; one-shot buffs stay armed until they fire.

2. BOSS INTENTS

  • Read the intent above the boss before ending your turn. Shield protects you; piercing damage bypasses it.
  • Some charged attacks show a STUN threshold: deal that much HP damage in one turn to cancel the intent. Miss it and the hit lands harder than shown.
  • Charm weakens enemy attacks; Marked makes the boss take extra damage. Curses and hazard rules can add special run challenges.

3. LEADERS & PROGRESSION

  • Choose Ahri, Akali, Evelynn, or Kai'Sa. Each has a once-per-run Leader Ability, a signature starter relic, and a 10-card starter deck leaning into their keyword.
  • Relics grant passive or triggered bonuses. Draft rewards shape your deck between waves; waves also pay Merch Tokens for the merch shop (cards, relics, removal, healing). Optional custom modifiers (Chaos Draft, No Cuts, Double Hazard) twist standard tours; Daily Tours always run clean.
  • Clear all 10 waves to unlock the next Encore tier. Art Studio unlocks MORE, FIRESTARTER, PRESTIGE, and animated ETERNAL looks; the Card Atelier spends Spotlight Shards on permanent card finishes; Missions pay Shards for feats; Daily runs can post to the community leaderboard.

6. STATUS EFFECTS & BUFFS

  • Charm: the boss deals 25% less damage while Charmed. Ticks down each turn.
  • Marked: the boss takes 50% extra damage while Marked. Ticks down each turn.
  • Weak: your Attacks deal 25% less damage while Weak. Ticks down each turn.
  • Encore: +1 damage on all your Attacks per stack. Kai'Sa cards reward holding it.
  • Strength: +1 damage on every boss hit per stack. Watch enrages and Skill-fed growth.
  • Holo Shield (Block): absorbs damage first. Yours decays by half each turn; the boss's resets each turn. Pierce bypasses it; Drain steals boss Block as your Shield.
  • Hype Energy: spend to play cards; unspent Energy is lost. Drained Energy cuts next turn's refill.
  • Crowd Cheer: charge to 100% to unleash the Joint Finale; falls when you take HP damage.
  • Retain / Ethereal / Exhaust: retained cards stay in hand; ethereal hazards burn you if still held at turn end, then exhaust; exhausted cards leave the fight.
  • Charged intents: deal the listed STUN damage in one turn to cancel them, or brace for a boosted hit.

7. BETWEEN WAVES

  • Backstage event first. Leona (or a stranger) offers a favour with a tradeoff — read both sides before picking; some options are hidden when impossible.
  • Pick a tour stop. Backstage Pass upgrades a card, Fan Meet drafts you a card, Merch Stand grants a relic. Card and relic rewards heal you; upgrading does not.
  • Spend Merch Tokens. The shop alongside the routing sells extra cards, a relic, deck removal, and healing. There is no free Cut or Rest — thinning and recovery cost tokens.

Keyboard shortcuts can be changed in Options. During a battle, press the configured key to play a card, end your turn, trigger Joint Finale, or use your Leader Ability.
